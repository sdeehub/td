1. Family comes first: make time for family, friends, partner, and children - Family first. Work second. 
2. Invest in yourself: Be a life-long learner - The highest ROI comes from investing in yourself.
3. Stop comparing yourself to others: focus on - your purpose, your experience, embracing your authenticity, and being true to yourself. *The only competition is staring back at you in the mirror.*
4. Find a support system: you are a product of your environment. Surround yourself with kind, honest, positive, energetic, and supportive people.
5. Be grateful for what you have: be thankful for - life, job, home, family, and health.
6. Accept your past and move on: stop getting stuck in your past. Learn from your past to make a better future. The present is the only place you can act, live, and love.
7. Respect yourself: people learn how to treat you based on how you treat yourself. How to respect yourself? Take action, accept yourself, and cut out negative people. You are in control of how you will be treated.
