What does Epictetus mean? Should we not strive for our goals and give up? - You should do both. He knew that life is out of control. No matter what we do we can't fully control outside circumstances. We can only attempt to control the circumstances and accept the rest.

Epictetus wants us to take action but not have an expectation to control the outcome of your action.

In today's world, we try to control every outcome. We want to study with a specific hack, we want to talk a certain way. We micromanage everything. We want events to happen as we picture them in our heads.

*My life changed when I stopped trying to make outcomes look the way I thought they should look.* With your emotions attached to your expectations, you block the path to getting what you want.

When you let go of your results looking a certain way, you accept there are many ways to get what you want.

You will see endless ways to make people like you, to win in the market, to study for the test.

*Best hack: Do your best and adapt to what happens!*

When you get out of tunnel vision - life will work for you, instead of you working for it.
